Model assumptions & what still needs to be established

*Endpoint definition: The proposed outcome is further confirmed tomographic progression after the selected visit. Thresholds, confirmation rules, rescue criteria and treatment eligibility must be prespecified before fitting a model.

What these curves are: Handcrafted cumulative risks. The benefit range is also preset; it is not obtained by subtracting the edges of two marginal ranges. Baseline and follow-up are separate illustrative scenarios, not updates from an inference engine.

What a real model needs: Longitudinal treatment and follow-up outcomes, explicit treatment strategies, suitable confounder adjustment, uncertainty estimation and independent validation. The existing NKPI CXL-associated score is not a causal treatment-effect estimate.

What is not modeled: Visual outcomes, complications, recovery burden, net clinical benefit or a treatment recommendation. A change between visits alone does not establish confirmed progression.
