What can and cannot be displayed?

Actual x/y/z samples support a measured surface patch. Anterior and posterior surfaces are separate; a missing surface is never manufactured. Supplied thickness, elevation and curvature grids may color those patches. Missing cells stay missing, without gap filling or extrapolation.

Elevation colors are residuals against the export’s declared reference; they are not used as absolute surface heights. No complete eyeball, posterior shell, curvature map or thickness map is estimated from summary values. The optional synthetic demo is visibly labelled and is never claimed to represent the current patient.

Numerical files stay in this browser component. This viewer sends only readiness and frame-height messages to its parent, never surface data. Use deidentified, institutionally approved exports. This is a research visualization, not a validated clinical reconstruction.
